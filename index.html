About Us

Adventure That Brings People Together

LakeSide Adventure Community is more than an adventure company — it is a people-powered, community-based eco-adventure and wellness movement. We bring together hikers, cyclists, nature lovers, and enthusiasts to explore, connect, and promote healthier lifestyles across Kisumu and East Africa.

🥾 Hiking

Mabungo Caves Adventure

Explore scenic trails, caves, and local landscapes with fellow adventure lovers.

  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Distance: 7 km
  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Price: KSh 1,800
🚴 Cycling

Kisumu Sunset Ride

Experience the beauty of Kisumu through an unforgettable sunrise or sunset cycle.

  • Distance: 15 km
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Start: Hippo Point
  • Price: KSh 2,200
🌅 Nature

Lake Experience & Picnic

Relax by the water, enjoy a picnic, and connect with nature in a calm and scenic setting.

  • Activity: Nature walk + picnic
  • Duration: 2–3 hours
  • Group: Small groups
  • Price: KSh 1,500

Weekend Community Meetup

Join friendly group activities, wellness moments, and meaningful connection with others.

  • Activities: Hike, networking, games
  • Age: 18+
  • Price: KSh 1,200
  • Meetup: Jomo Kenyatta Sports Ground
